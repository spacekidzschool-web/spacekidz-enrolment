📄 Enrolment & Indemnity Agreement

Please read the full agreement below before accepting.

1. Acknowledgement & Acceptance

1.1 The Parent(s)/Guardian(s) accept and agree to abide by all rules, regulations, and conditions of the School, with which they declare themselves fully acquainted.

1.2 The School and its employees will care for the child to the best of their ability but will not accept liability for any accident, injury, loss, or damages arising while the child attends the School, except in cases of gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

1.3 The Parent(s)/Guardian(s) indemnify the School and its employees against any claims that may arise or be instituted in respect of the child, save where South African law provides otherwise.

2. Health & Medical

2.1 The Parent(s)/Guardian(s) confirm the child has been properly immunised against Whooping Cough, Diphtheria, Tetanus, Polio, and Tuberculosis, and will provide proof on request.

2.2 In an emergency, the School supervisor or another responsible person is authorised to consent to medical treatment or surgery for the child, under the supervision of a medical doctor.

2.3 The Parent(s)/Guardian(s) accept responsibility for all medical expenses incurred.

3. Illness & Exclusion Policy

3.1 A child with a fever, vomiting, diarrhoea, or symptoms of a contagious illness or unexplained rash may not attend the School.

3.2 A child sent home due to illness must be fever-free (without fever-reducing medication) and symptom-free for a minimum of 24 hours before returning.

3.3 The School may send a child home who becomes ill at School; reasonable additional transport/supervision costs may be recovered from the Parent(s)/Guardian(s).

3.4 Fees remain payable in full during any period of exclusion due to illness.

4. Behaviour, Discipline & Parent Cooperation

4.1 The School follows a structured, age-appropriate discipline system to guide and encourage well-behaved conduct and help each child follow the School's and classroom's rules.

In practice, this includes:

  • A calm, brief time-out (no playtime), explained to the child, matched to the child's age in minutes.
  • A rewards chart — children earn stars for good behaviour, helping others, completing work, and following instructions.
  • A star may be removed for correction; if none remain, a further brief time-out applies and ends once complete.
  • 10 stars earns a small reward, after which the chart resets.
  • Discipline always leads with positive reinforcement, is age-appropriate, and is never physical or harsh.

4.2 Where a child displays disruptive or unsafe behaviour, the School will always try to manage this internally first, before contacting the Parent(s)/Guardian(s).

4.3 If informed of disruptive behaviour, the Parent(s)/Guardian(s) agree to cooperate fully with the School, and not to hold the School, its teachers, or staff responsible, given the consistent system applied to all learners.

4.4 Children may not, under any circumstances, physically or verbally abuse teachers, staff, or other children, or damage School property. Abusive conduct by a Parent/Guardian toward staff or other children will also not be tolerated.

4.5 Should disruptive behaviour persist, or a Parent/Guardian behave as described above, the School may require the child's permanent removal — with no refund of any fees paid, under any circumstances.

5. Fees & Payment

5.1 A non-refundable registration fee of R900 is payable upon enrolment.

5.2 Monthly tuition fees are payable in advance by the last day of the preceding month.

5.3 Payments received after the 5th of the month attract an automatic R220 late-payment penalty.

5.4 Non-payment may result in suspension of services and/or legal action to recover amounts owed.

5.5 Fees remain payable regardless of absence, and during the December closure period (payable in advance, deductible from annual totals but not refundable).

5.6 Should any account become overdue and be handed over for collection, the Parent(s)/Guardian(s) agree to be liable for all collection costs, tracing fees, commission charged by the debt collection agency (Accountability, the School's appointed agent), legal costs on the attorney-and-client scale where applicable, and interest on overdue amounts at the maximum rate permitted by law under the Prescribed Rate of Interest Act 55 of 1975. These costs are added to and payable as part of the outstanding account, in addition to the amount originally owed.

5.7 Fees may be reviewed annually at the School's discretion, with a minimum of 30 days' written notice before any increase takes effect. A sibling or other discount may be offered at the School's sole discretion and is not a guaranteed entitlement unless confirmed in writing.

6. Operating Hours, Holidays & Late Collection

Monday–Thursday 06:15–17:30, Friday 06:15–17:00. Closed on all SA public holidays (plus the adjoining Monday/Friday where a holiday falls on a Tuesday/Thursday). The School generally closes around 15 December and reopens the first week of January. A late collection fee of R100 per half hour (or part thereof) applies after closing time.

7. Termination of Enrolment

One full calendar month's written notice is required. No withdrawal notices are accepted for October, November, or December. Failure to give notice results in liability for one month's fees in lieu of notice.

8. Participation in School Events

A child may not participate in the year-end concert or graduation ceremony if fees are outstanding, or if enrolment ends in November without proper notice.

9. Photography & Social Media Consent

9.1 The School may photograph/video children during activities for its own records, classroom displays, and promotional material including its website and social media.

9.2 The School will not publish a child's full name alongside their photograph without separate written consent.

10. POPIA Compliance

The Parent(s)/Guardian(s) consent to the School collecting and processing their and their child's personal information, in accordance with POPIA, for enrolment, administration, and communication purposes.

11. General

This Agreement is binding on both parties and their successors, governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both the School and the Parent(s)/Guardian(s).
